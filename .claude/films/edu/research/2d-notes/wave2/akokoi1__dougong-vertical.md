## What I saw
- 3D, not 2D: a warm low-key studio scene in near-black brown with amber light. Lacquered red columns, a white lotus plinth, golden-orange
  bracket sets (斗拱), a slate roof plane, all simple solid-coloured geometry with soft shadows. The camera moves in every shot: the opening
  flies through the bracket beams, close-ups orbit the column head, then it pulls out to the whole bay.
- **Hook, 0-3 s**: the camera is already moving on frame 1. A huge gold "悬空 4 米" (heavy sans, with the numeral in white) sits in the upper
  third. On the reveal it adds "下面 一根柱子都没有" (red emphasis), and a translucent ghost pillar with a red X marks the missing support under the
  4 m eave. The subtitle repeats the line: "屋檐悬空伸出将近四米，下面一根柱子都没有。"
- A real asset: Liang Sicheng's 1940s hand-drawn plate of "THE CHINESE ORDER" appears as an aged-paper card with a gold name tag
  "梁思成 LIANG SICHENG · 1901-1972". The 3D then rebuilds that plate: Western orders (Doric, Ionic, Corinthian columns) -> "中国的“柱式”"
  wireframe -> 01 柱础 BASE -> 02 柱 COLUMN -> 03 斗拱 TOU-KUNG -> 04 出跳 T'IAO (numbered ①②③ tiers) -> 05 昂 ANG (lever with a fulcrum: "一压 · 一挑 = 平衡",
  "屋顶重量压住") -> 06 材 TS'AI (the module unit: "1 材 = 15 分 × 10 分", a 3:2 beam cut from a log, "材 3:2 = 1.50", eight grades with a 1.7 m human
  for scale) -> a full bay with numbered parts -> the end card "中国建筑之“柱式” THE CHINESE ORDER" plus a question chip **"你数出来了吗? 3 跳"**
  (it answers the counting question asked earlier: built-in comment bait and a rewatch hook).
- Typography: gold chapter chips top left ("03 斗拱 TOU-KUNG", with Wade-Giles romanisation as in Liang's plate); bilingual labels with
  numbered gold pins on parts ("24 柱 COLUMN", "35 栌斗 LU-TOU"); a thin gold progress bar at the very top edge. Subtitles are two lines: white
  Chinese with gold keywords (四米 / 梁思成 / 柱础 / 斗拱), and a smaller grey English line underneath. The subtitle sits at about 88-90% of frame
  height, which is inside the bottom area that Douyin's caption and username overlay usually covers (the dylantlwu course gives 170 px of 1920 as
  unsafe, so this is borderline).
- Overall it feels like a museum display or a model kit. It is far less slide-like than the 16:9 dotey and atmosphere videos because the
  camera keeps moving through one object that gets assembled, rather than switching between diagram pages.
