The character is "Muse", an original mascot of our own design (not based on any existing character, game or brand): a small, plump, rounded spirit about two heads tall, shaped like a soft teardrop with stubby round arms and two tiny feet. Smooth lavender-violet skin (#9C8CFF) with a pale lilac belly, big friendly dark eyes with two white highlights each, small rosy cheeks, a tiny smile. On top of its head a small glowing golden spark (a four-pointed star on a short stem) as its antenna. It wears a small amber-orange miner's helmet with a round warm headlamp, and a short coral-red scarf. No green anywhere on the character.
