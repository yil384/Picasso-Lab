## What I saw
- 0-2 s: closed red velvet curtains with a scalloped valance; they part to reveal a peach dusk sky.
- One locked stage for the whole film (a proscenium: curtain strips stay at both edges and the valance on top). Rolling
  hills, round trees and a town skyline as layered silhouettes; a sun sets, the sky goes peach -> rose -> violet ->
  indigo; stars and a cratered moon appear.
- A dark sign board hangs on two strings from the valance, dotted border, cream serif italic text ("What makes a place /
  feel like home?", "Not the roof. / Not the walls.", "It's a light / left on for you.", "Something warm / on the stove.",
  "Someone who / knows your name.", "Small things, repeated, / until they're yours.", "A house is where / you live.",
  "A home is what / waits for you there."). Text types on letter by letter.
- The house rises in as a silhouette; windows light warm yellow (a pot on the stove in one, a cat in the other), smoke
  curls, a scarecrow-like figure in a top hat, an arc of steam/scent travels from chimney to figure, a second figure
  arrives and the door opens with a trapezoid of light spilling onto the ground (the best frame). A bare tree, birds,
  a lamp post, a windmill.
- ~44-48 s: the only camera move, a slow pull-back that reveals more lit houses; fireflies. 55-57 s: curtains close.
- Full res: soft vignette, a fine paper grain on the sky, silhouettes with slightly irregular edges, warm glow halos
  around windows. Pleasant, but the silhouettes are simple shapes and the figures barely act.

## 9:16 composition as made (estimated from the 720x1280 file, scaled to 1080x1920)
- Caption sign at roughly y 200-445, text about 60 px tall lines at 1080 width, centred; i.e. in the top band.
  That sits under Instagram's top overlay band (0-420 per the ClaudeAnimationBase fork) but clear of YouTube Shorts'
  ~180 px top bar.
- Subject (house) centred at x 540, roughly y 975-1560; the bottom ~20% is a dark empty ground band (unintentionally
  safe for the bottom UI).
- The proscenium curtains eat ~60 px each side: a cheap way to frame a tall image as "a stage".

## Quality (candid): 6/10
A one-shot prompt that lands mood, palette and a coherent idea in 15 minutes. As an explainer it is a poem: captions
carry the content, the picture illustrates it; the camera is locked for ~55 s, the beats are additive (one more prop
lights up per line) and the pacing is slow. Reach was tiny.

## Borrow
- The theatre frame as a format for a vertical channel: a fixed proscenium + hanging caption sign is a strong, reusable
  "set" that makes a locked camera feel intentional, and gives captions a physical home instead of a subtitle bar.
- Light as the payoff (the door opening, light spilling); one colour arc (dusk -> night) for the whole piece.
- Proof of what a bare one-shot prompt gives: a baseline to beat.
## Avoid
- Locked camera + additive props for a whole minute; captions in the platform's top overlay zone; figures that do not act.
