The stream UI's assets (src/ui-panel.js, src/camera.js):

- sprites/, icons/: the "Muse" mascot and the UI icons, our own original art, made with Codex's image tool from the
  prompts in the episode's ui-art folder (gen.sh, character.txt, style.txt) on a #00FF00 screen, keyed by ui-art/key.mjs.
- fonts/Inter-*.ttf: Inter 4.1 by The Inter Project Authors (github.com/rsms/inter), SIL Open Font License 1.1
  (fonts/Inter-OFL.txt).
- fonts/JetBrainsMono-*.ttf: JetBrains Mono 2.304 by The JetBrains Mono Project Authors (github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono),
  SIL Open Font License 1.1 (fonts/JetBrainsMono-OFL.txt); the raw calls in the HUD.
- music/etirwer.m4a: "Etirwer" by Kistol (opengameart.org/content/etirwer), CC0 1.0, a seamless game loop; levelled
  in ~/picasso-work/game-music. Credit not required; given here anyway.
